What this notice coversAbin da wannan sanarwa ta hada

This notice explains, in plain language, how We Aid Initiative handles information collected through this website.Wannan sanarwa ta bayyana, a sauki harshe, yadda We Aid Initiative ke gudanar da bayanan da aka tara ta wannan rukunin yanar gizo.

What we collectAbin da muke tarawa

  • Information you submit: name, email and message content when you use the contact form, the volunteer form or The Citizen reportingRahoton 'yan kasa form (unless you choose to submit anonymously).
  • Technical information: browser type, device, approximate location and pages visited. Used only to improve the site.

What we do NOT doAbin da BA MA yi

  • We do not sell your personal data.Ba mu sayar da bayanan ka na sirri.
  • We do not share Citizen reports with anyone outside our governance team without your consent.Ba mu raba rahotannin Mai Kasa da kowa a wajen tawagar shugabancinmu ba tare da yardar ka ba.
  • We do not run advertising on this site.Ba ma gudanar da tallace-tallace a wannan rukunin yanar gizo.

Your rightsHakkokin ka

You can request a copy of data we hold about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted.Za ka iya neman kwafin bayanan da muka rike kanka, tambayi a gyara su, ko tambayi a goge su. EmailImel info@weaidinitiative.org.

CookiesKuki

This site uses minimal cookies — only what is needed to remember your language preference and (when implemented) any session you have with The Citizen.Wannan rukunin yanar gizo yana amfani da kuki kadan — kawai abin da ake bukata don tunawa da zabin harshenka da (idan an aiwatar) kowane zama da kake da Mai Kasa.
