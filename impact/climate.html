What we do hereAbin da muke yi a nan

Locally-grounded climate adaptation, urban farming, waste-to-energy briquette production and tree planting — alongside policy advocacy on environmental protection.Daidaitawa da yanayi a matakin gida, noma a birni, samar da briquette daga sharar gida da dasa bishiyoyi — tare da kira-zuwa-gaba na manufofi kan kare muhalli.

What this looks like in practiceYadda wannan ke kasancewa a aikace

In partnership with the Bauchi State Environmental Protection Agency (BASEPA), We Aid Initiative ran a Green Horizons tree-planting exercise to promote environmental conservation and combat climate change.A hadin gwiwa da Hukumar Kare Muhalli ta Jihar Bauchi (BASEPA), We Aid Initiative ya gudanar da motsa jiki na dasa bishiyoyi na Green Horizons don ciyar da kiyaye muhalli da yakar sauyin yanayi.
