Education & EntrepreneurshipIlimi & Sana'a

Turning learning into livelihoods.Juya ilimi zuwa rayuwa.

Education is a right — and a route out of poverty. We work with girls, women and out-of-school youth to make learning accessible, useful and economically meaningful.Ilimi haƙƙi ne — kuma hanya ce ta fita daga talauci. Muna aiki tare da yara mata, mata da matasa marasa makaranta don sa ilimi ya zama mai sauƙi, mai amfani da kuma mai ma'anar tattalin arziki.

What we do hereAbin da muke yi a nan

Our education and entrepreneurship work targets the points where learning meets opportunity: girls who would otherwise drop out, women who need a craft to support their families, and young people who left school without the skills the economy now demands.Aikinmu na ilimi da sana'a yana mai da hankali kan inda ilimi ke haɗuwa da dama: yara mata waɗanda za su daina karatu, mata waɗanda suke buƙatar sana'a don tallafa wa iyalansu, da matasa waɗanda suka bar makaranta ba tare da kwarewar da tattalin arziki ke buƙata yanzu ba.

Programme componentsBangarorin shirin

  • Yar Boko — girl-child education and awareness on the right of every child to go to school. Includes Learn to Read and Exam Ready tracks.ilimin yara mata da wayar da kai kan haƙƙin kowane yaro ya zuwa makaranta. Ya haɗa da Learn to Read da Exam Ready.
  • Women's skills training and craft pathways — sewing, baking, soap-making, cosmetics, agro-processing.Horon kwarewa ga mata da hanyoyin sana'a — ɗinki, gasa, sabulu, kayan kwalliya, sarrafa albarkatun gona.
  • Adult literacy classes in Hausa and English.Karatun manya a Hausa da Turanci.
  • Enterprise mentorship — book-keeping, pricing, market access, registering a business.Jagorancin kasuwanci — adana littattafan kuɗi, farashi, samun kasuwa, yin rajistar kasuwanci.
  • Civic-tech and digital-skills tracks for out-of-school youth.Hanyoyin fasaha da kwarewa na dijital ga matasa marasa makaranta.

What this looks like in practiceYadda wannan ke kasancewa a aikace

Through Yar Boko, we run school-readiness, literacy and exam-preparation sessions for girls in rural Bauchi communities — many of whom are first-generation learners. Alongside, our women's enterprise pathways have trained women in their craft of choice so they can earn a living and contribute to community life.Ta hanyar Yar Boko, muna gudanar da darussan shirye-shiryen makaranta, karatu da shirye-shiryen jarrabawa ga 'yan mata a al'ummomin karkara na Bauchi.

"Entrepreneurship is key to sustainable living. We provide women with training of their choice to help them earn a living and contribute to society.""Sana'a tushen rayuwa mai dorewa ce. Muna ba mata horon zaɓinsu don taimaka musu su sami abin rayuwa kuma su ba da gudummawa ga al'umma."
