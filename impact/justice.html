What this looks like in practiceYadda wannan ke kasancewa a aikace

On 25 July 2024 we engaged stakeholders in Bauchi State to establish a Justice Center for Gender-Based Violence focused on prompt reporting, survivor referral, and collective action against GBV. The founder also represented the organisation at the GBV in Emergencies training at the American University of Nigeria.A ranar 25 ga Yuli 2024 mun hada masu ruwa da tsaki a Jihar Bauchi don kafa Cibiyar Adalci na Cin Zarafin Jinsi da aka mayar da hankali kan bayar da rahoto cikin sauri, tura wadanda suka tsira, da hadin gwiwa kan GBV. Wanda ya kafa kuma ya wakilci kungiyar a horon GBV a Yanayin Gaggawa a Jami'ar Amurka ta Najeriya.
