Use this form to flag a public-service problem in your community. Submissions are reviewed by our governance team and routed to the relevant authorities.Yi amfani da wannan fom don bayyana matsalar hidima ta jama'a a cikin al'ummarka. Do not include personally identifying information about victims without consent.Kada ka hada bayanai na ainihi kan wadanda abin ya shafa ba tare da yardarsu ba.

What happens after you submitAbin da ke faruwa bayan ka aiko

  1. You receive a unique tracking ID (format CTZ-YYYYMM-XXXXX).
  2. Our governance team reviews the report within 5 business days.Tawagar shugabancinmu na dubawa rahoton cikin kwanaki 5 na aiki.
  3. If actionable, the report is escalated to the appropriate authority and an update is posted.Idan ya cancanci aiki, ana kara rahoton ga hukumar da ta dace kuma ana wallafa sabuntawa.
  4. The status of every report is visible on the public dashboard.

SafeguardsKariya

  • Anonymous submissions are accepted.Ana karbar aikawa ba tare da suna ba.
  • Sensitive cases (GBV, violence) are routed to specialised partners.Ana tura lamuran masu hassasiyya (GBV, tashin hankali) ga abokan hadin gwiwa kwararru.
  • Personal data is never published.Ba a wallafa bayanai na sirri.

ℹ️ Submitted reports are sent securely to our governance team and also displayed on the public dashboard with your tracking ID. Personal data is never published.Ana aika rahotannin da aka aiko cikin tsaro ga tawagar shugabancinmu kuma ana nuna su a kan allon jama'a tare da lambar bin diddiginka.
