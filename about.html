About UsGame da Mu

Citizens at the centre.'Yan kasa a tsakiyar al'amura.

Founded in Bauchi State by Barr. Gambo Yakubu Wakili, We Aid Initiative is a Nigerian civil-society organisation working on participatory governance, gender justice, climate action and policy innovation across the country.An kafa ta a Jihar Bauchi ta Barr. Gambo Yakubu Wakili, We Aid Initiative kungiya ce ta jama'a a Najeriya wadda ke aiki kan shugabanci na hadin gwiwa, adalcin jinsi, aikin sauyin yanayi da kirkire-kirkiren manufofi a fadin kasar.

Who we areWanene mu

A Nigerian civic-society organisation working where governance, justice and the environment meet.Kungiyar jama'a ta Najeriya da ke aiki inda shugabanci, adalci da muhalli suka hadu.

We Aid Initiative was founded by Barr. Gambo Yakubu Wakili and is registered as a Nigerian civil-society organisation operating from Bauchi State and working nationally.

Our mission is to galvanize stakeholder commitment and action towards achieving the Sustainable Development GoalsManufa (SDGs) in Africa, through advocacy, community projects and partnerships that create sustainable impact.

Our seven focus areas are Governance, Human Rights, Inclusion, Youth Leadership, Peace and Conflict Resolution, Health Care, and Education and Entrepreneurship — each anchored in our commitment to the Sustainable Development Goals.

"A citizen-centered innovation, governance and policy organisation using civic engagement, technology, justice, climate action and community participation to drive sustainable and democratic development."

A world of full human rights for every person.Duniya inda kowane mutum ke da cikakkun hakkokin dan adam.

To envision a world where every individual, regardless of gender, race or background, enjoys full human rights, equal opportunities, and active participation in shaping inclusive and sustainable societies.Mu hango duniyar da kowane mutum, ba tare da la'akari da jinsi, kabila ko asali ba, yake jin daɗin cikakkun hakkokin dan adam, daidaitattun damammaki, da kuma shiga aiki wajen tsara al'ummomi masu hada kai da dorewa.
