Citizens at the centre.'Yan kasa a tsakiyar al'amura.

Founded in Bauchi State by Barr. Gambo Yakubu Wakili, We Aid Initiative is a Nigerian civil-society organisation working on participatory governance, gender justice, climate action and policy innovation across the country.An kafa ta a Jihar Bauchi ta Barr. Gambo Yakubu Wakili, We Aid Initiative kungiya ce ta jama'a a Najeriya wadda ke aiki kan shugabanci na hadin gwiwa, adalcin jinsi, aikin sauyin yanayi da kirkire-kirkiren manufofi a fadin kasar.

Who we areWanene mu

A Nigerian civic-society organisation working where governance, justice and the environment meet.Kungiyar jama'a ta Najeriya da ke aiki inda shugabanci, adalci da muhalli suka hadu.

We Aid Initiative was founded by Barr. Gambo Yakubu Wakili and is registered as a Nigerian civil-society organisation operating from Bauchi State and working nationally.

Our mission is to galvanize stakeholder commitment and action towards achieving the Sustainable Development GoalsManufa (SDGs) in Africa, through advocacy, community projects and partnerships that create sustainable impact.

We work across six interlocking impact areas: governance and civic engagement, justice and gender protection, climate action and environment, human rights and legal empowerment, policy and research, and innovation and youth development.

"A citizen-centered innovation, governance and policy organisation using civic engagement, technology, justice, climate action and community participation to drive sustainable and democratic development."

Founder & Executive Director

Barr. Gambo (Yakubu) Wakili

Gambo (Yakubu) Wakili is a legal practitioner, governance and inclusion advocate, policy strategist, and energy consultant with a strong commitment to social impact and institutional reform in Nigeria.Gambo (Yakubu) Wakili lauya ce, mai bayar da shawara kan shugabanci da hada kai, mai tsara manufofi, kuma mai ba da shawara kan makamashi mai kuduri kan tasirin zamantakewa da kawo sauyi a Najeriya.

She is the Founder and Executive Director of We Aid Initiative, where she leads programs focused on inclusion, education, civic empowerment, and sustainable development. With extensive legal experience from her work at Law Corridor Abuja, she has built a solid foundation in legal practice and advisory services.Ita ce Wadda ta kafa kuma Darektan Zartarwa na We Aid Initiative, inda take jagorantar shirye-shirye kan hada kai, ilimi, karfafa 'yan kasa, da ci gaba mai dorewa. Tare da gogewa mai yawa daga aikinta a Law Corridor Abuja, ta gina tushe mai karfi a aikin shari'a da bayar da shawara.

Gambo's work bridges law, governance, and development — supporting policies and initiatives that advance equity, strengthen public institutions, and promote community-driven change across diverse sectors, including energy and education.Aikin Gambo yana hade shari'a, shugabanci da ci gaba — yana goyon bayan manufofi da shirye-shirye da ke ciyar da daidaito gaba, karfafa cibiyoyin gwamnati, da inganta sauyi karkashin jagorancin al'umma a sassa daban-daban da suka hada da makamashi da ilimi.
